# Four-player playtest, 2026-11-27/28

Two runs, four players, Steam, Normal, host on an RTX 4060 Ti (mean 101-110 fps, no samples under 40).
Raw logs stay local in `Builds/playtests/` (they carry player names). `python tools/run_report.py Builds/playtests` prints the tables.

## What the logs say

| Run | Party | Result | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Knight, Plague Doctor, Ranger, Ranger | wiped on floor 5 (The Broken Court) | 77 min |
| 2 | Flagellant, Plague Doctor, Mage, Rogue | wiped on floor 7 (The Nave), at the Choirmaster | 85 min |

- Run 1 floor 5: 19 of 23 downs came from **The Twice-Crowned** (a lair miniboss, not the guardian) in one Great Hall over ~3 minutes; it dealt 6,673 to players, more than any other source across both runs. Cause in code: its shadow twin ran the whole boss routine too (hunting strikes, lance fans, shared doom), so the party faced two bosses' worth of ground attacks; lances were 24 m long and reached past the walls; ground strikes had a 0.85 s tell.
- Run 2 floor 7: 11 downs at the Choirmaster, 4 at Mother Veyle (lair).
- Traps: spike plates and "traps and afflictions" are the second and third biggest sources of player damage in run 1 (5 downs to spike plates).
- Damage spread: the two Rangers in run 1 dealt 5-13% each on floors 1-2; one Ranger dealt 2,898 on floor 2 (5%).
- Unused all run: arena engines, cellar hatches, hand torches, hearth braziers, puzzle statues, quest cargo (run 1); expedition remains, oath stone, reliquary (run 2).

## Changes made from this playtest

- Lair minibosses: echoes no longer hunt or lay dooms; hunting strikes and lance fans do half damage; every boss's ground strikes have a longer tell (1.15 s) and the circle under YOU is brighter with a warning sound (`BossBrain.StrikeCircleRpc`); boss lances stop at the navmesh edge (`LanceReach`); lair attacks only reach players inside the lair room (`LairBrain.Reaches`). Twice-Crowned twin charge: every 13 s (was 9), x1.0 damage (was 1.3), one hit per player when both lanes cross.
- A player an ally revives gets 1.5 s of invulnerability (`PlayerLife.ReviveGrace`); two revivers now revive twice as fast (`TwoHandsRate` 2).
- Trip wires: blast 50% of max health (was 35%).
- No fall damage when landing in water (`DungeonDirector.LandsInWater`).
- No flame vents on flooded floors.
- Relic finds and the reliquary reward also go to chat.
- Esc while at blackjack closes the table without opening the pause menu.
- Tab sheet: THIS RUN shows each player's class.
- Title "the Maidenless".
- Camp: the gaming tent roof is solid; the crates behind the archery targets are stacked (two used to float); anyone below the camp floor is put back at spawn.
- Tavern band falls off sooner (heard to 24 m, was 42). Bell frame: the ringer hears their bell at once.
- Economy: dungeon gold x0.54 (was 0.62), embers x0.72 (was 0.62).
