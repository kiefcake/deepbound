# Deepbound

A 1–4 player first-person co-op dungeon crawler with a small-pixel, retro gothic look. Pick one of seven classes,
descend ten floors of procedurally built crypts with your friends, and break the guardians at the bottom of each act.
Everything you find is gone when the run ends; what you unlock along the way (cosmetics, records, journal pages)
stays.

Built in **Unity 6000.3.23f1** (URP, Forward+) with **Netcode for GameObjects**, playable over **Steam** (lobbies and
invites) or by direct address. The meshes, textures, materials and animation are generated by code; the sound effects,
music and room tone are CC0 recordings (see the credits), apart from the tavern band's original songs.

---

## The loop

```
CASTLE RAVENHOLD (camp)  ──carriage──▶  FLOOR  ──▶  GUARDIAN  ──grave lift──▶  back to camp
        ▲                                                                          │
        └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                         five acts, two floors each, ten floors in all
```

- **Camp** is a walled courtyard between floors: shops, the smith's anvil, the runewright's stones, a shrine, a
  training yard and archery range, a gambling tent (dice, slots, blackjack against a skeleton), the **Gutted Lantern**
  tavern (mead and a band you can pay for a song), a tailor for cosmetics, a bounty board, the Omen Stone, and a cat.
  When everyone sits on Corwin's carriage, the party sets off.
- **Floors** are seeded room graphs with special rooms (crypts, chasms, cisterns, libraries, gauntlets, vaults behind
  three levers, shrines, a reliquary to carry home), traps, secrets, champions, elites with affixes, and floor events.
- **Guardians** end each floor: the first floor of every act ends at a Warden, the second at the act's master. Bosses
  have phases, break meters, weak points and mechanics to solve together (shared doom circles, decrees, survival
  phases, wards).
- **Acts:** The Ossuary · The Drowned Vaults · The Hollow Throne · The Black Cathedral · and, if the party broke the
  seals on the way, The Abyss.
- **Difficulty:** Normal, Hard, Nightmare, and Torment (unlocked by finishing a descent). Enemy health and numbers scale
  with party size, and rescale if someone joins or leaves mid-floor.

## Classes

| Class | Role | Kit in a line |
|---|---|---|
| **Knight** | Tank | Sword and shield: block from the front, Shield Bash stuns, War Cry taunts |
| **Mage** | Burst | Firebolt, Frostbolt that chills for Shatter, a stunning Tidal Wave, Meteor |
| **Cleric** | Healer | Smite, a healing beam, Consecrate, Aegis shields for the party |
| **Rogue** | Assassin | Fast stabs, fan of knives, Shadowstep behind a target, Smoke Bomb to vanish |
| **Ranger** | Marksman | Charged bow shots with drop, Disengage, a net-bursting Snare Shot, Volley |
| **Flagellant** | Bruiser | Hits harder and heals the more hurt it is; Scourge, Chains, Penitent Leap |
| **Plague Doctor** | Afflicter / support | Blighting vials, healing tonics, Miasma, the Plague Mask |

Every class has an ultimate, a weapon to temper (+10, with paths and edges), runes, ability ranks, relics, armour and
charms, a potion pouch, and a kick.

## Playing with friends

See [`docs/PLAY_WITH_FRIENDS.txt`](docs/PLAY_WITH_FRIENDS.txt). In short: everyone runs Steam, the host presses PLAY and
invites from the pause menu (Esc); friends accept the invite. Proximity voice chat, text chat, pings (Z / hold for the
ping wheel) and emotes (H / hold for the emote wheel) are built in. Downed players can be revived (hold F) or dragged
to safety (tap F); a player who bleeds out leaves a soul skull to carry back to the waystone.

## Building

Install Unity **6000.3.23f1**, clone the repo and open the folder once in Unity (or just run the build below). The
whole game — art, prefabs, the scene and the Windows player — is produced by one editor method:

```bash
"C:\Program Files\Unity\Hub\Editor\6000.3.23f1\Editor\Unity.exe" -batchmode -quit -projectPath . -executeMethod Deepbound.EditorTools.GameBuilder.BuildWithPlayer -logFile Logs/game-build.log
```

The player lands in `Builds/Deepbound/Deepbound.exe` (Windows, DirectX 11). Do not hand-edit `Assets/Prefabs`,
`Assets/Scenes`, `Assets/Resources/Materials` or `Assets/Art/Generated`: they are regenerated by the build.

## Testing

- **Smoke test** (headless, host + client over localhost; the main regression check, ~200 checks):
  ```bash
  cd Builds/Deepbound
  ./Deepbound.exe -batchmode -nographics -port 7791 -host -smoketest 40 -logFile host.log &
  sleep 4
  ./Deepbound.exe -batchmode -nographics -port 7791 -client -smoketest 34 -logFile client.log
  grep -E "FAIL|Exception" host.log client.log
  ```
- **Screenshots:** `Deepbound.exe -shots <dir> -screen-fullscreen 0 -screen-width 1280 -screen-height 720`
- **Boss test:** `Deepbound.exe -bosstest <dir>` walks every boss through its phases.
- **Autoplay:** `Deepbound.exe -autoplay <dir> [-autoclass Mage] [-autodepth N]` — a bot plays a whole run and logs
  everything it sees.

Tests never touch save data.
